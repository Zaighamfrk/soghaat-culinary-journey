# Soghaat Culinary Journey

Create a premium, highly interactive restaurant website for **Soghaat Cuisine**, a Pakistani/desi restaurant in Multan, Pakistan.

## Brand & Restaurant

Restaurant name: **Soghaat Cuisine**

Location: **A Block Gulgasht Colony 34A, Multan, Pakistan**

Google Maps location:
https://maps.app.goo.gl/4dfjR8ijgsqk1oXj9

Resolved Google Maps coordinates:
**30.2165112, 71.4701141**

Instagram:
https://www.instagram.com/soghaatcuisine/

Use the Instagram account as the primary visual reference for the restaurant's food photography, presentation, branding, colors, atmosphere and menu. Do NOT invent dishes, prices, restaurant claims, reviews, opening hours, phone numbers or other business information. If information cannot be verified, leave it out or use a clearly marked placeholder.

## Overall Design Direction

Build a sophisticated Pakistani restaurant website that feels premium, warm, authentic and appetizing.

The visual identity should combine:

* Traditional Pakistani culinary heritage
* Modern luxury restaurant design
* Warm earthy tones
* Elegant typography
* High-quality food photography
* Subtle Pakistani/Islamic geometric patterns
* Rich but restrained animations
* Plenty of whitespace
* Premium editorial-style layouts

Avoid making it look like a generic fast-food website.

Suggested visual direction:

* Deep charcoal / near-black background sections
* Warm cream / ivory backgrounds
* Subtle gold or bronze accents
* Rich food photography
* Elegant serif headings combined with a clean modern sans-serif
* Soft shadows
* Rounded but sophisticated cards
* Subtle grain/texture in selected sections

The website must be fully responsive and optimized for mobile, tablet and desktop.
